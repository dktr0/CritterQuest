Critter Quest is the game created by everyone in the Media Arts Game Design course (MEDIAART 3L03, taught by David Ogborn) at McMaster University (Winter 2023). Wander through diverse biomes while attempting to bring a series of animal friends back to where they want/need to be! Created with Godot game engine and Blender.
Asset Designers: Erinma Arki, Avis Bomberry, Rebecca Charlton, Hala Jaber, Konatsu Wang, Keiko Kushibiki, Anusha Menon, Nada Ayoub, Naomi Redwood, Jack Le Tran, Onai White, Shiqi Yu, Jiawei Wu
